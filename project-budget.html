Personal Budget Management

A Windows desktop application for entering expenses, configuring category budgets, and reviewing spending with a transaction table and pie chart.

What the project does

  • Record expense dates, amounts and categories.
  • Search transaction history within a selected date range.
  • Review category spending through a pie chart.
  • Store transactions and budget settings locally in CSV files.
TechnologiesC# · Windows Forms · .NET Framework · CSV
